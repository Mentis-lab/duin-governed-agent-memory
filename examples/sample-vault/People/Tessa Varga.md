# Tessa Varga

Founder of Kestrel Labs and hardware lead. Tessa designs the Skyline main board, owns the bill of materials and the budget, and is the studio's contact for [[Halvard Brenna]] at Greyfell Assembly and for [[Corinne Abelard]] at the Lodestar Open Hardware Fund.

## Owns
- [[Skyline v1 Hardware]]: board revisions A through C, power budget, sensor selection
- The v1 program budget ($61,000 plan, currently forecast at $68,300)
- The relationship with Greyfell Assembly and the rev C delivery

## Positions she has taken
- Argued for MIT on everything in [[2026-03-19-open-source-license]], to make adoption frictionless. Lost the hardware half of that argument to Anouk's case for a reciprocal license and says she is glad she did.
- Pushed for a custom main board over a dev-kit carrier in [[2026-04-09-custom-main-board]].
- Backed Rafael's field data over Ines's simplicity argument in [[2026-05-14-switch-to-lora]].
- Chose 40 kits over 100 in [[2026-07-02-beta-batch-size]] to keep the overrun inside the studio reserve.

## Current commitments
- Second-source samples of the LoRa module in hand by 2026-09-05 (from [[2026-08-06-supplier-delay-escalation]])
- Second-batch recommendation, with Halvard's quote, to the team by 2026-09-12
- Assemble and test 40 kits with Rafael between 2026-09-19 and 2026-09-26
- Draft the extension request to Corinne if the beta cannot start by 2026-10-31
