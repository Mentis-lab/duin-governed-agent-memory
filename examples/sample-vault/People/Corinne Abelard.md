# Corinne Abelard

Program officer at the Lodestar Open Hardware Fund, which is funding the Skyline v1 program. Corinne manages the grant, reviews the milestone reports and decides whether tranche 2 is released.

## The grant
- Total award $88,000 in two tranches, agreed at [[2026-04-02-lodestar-grant-call]]
- Tranche 1, $48,000, paid 2026-04-20; it funds the 40-kit beta and the rev C boards
- Tranche 2, $40,000, conditional on a beta report by 2026-11-30 showing at least 30 stations reporting for four consecutive weeks
- The fund requires the hardware to be released under a recognised open hardware license, which [[2026-03-19-open-source-license]] satisfied

## What she has said
- She prefers an honest report about a late beta over a rushed one. She said this on 2026-04-02 and repeated it in an email on 2026-08-14 after [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]].
- A launch later than 2026-10-31 would make a 2026-11-30 report impossible, since it needs four weeks of station data. An extension has to be asked for in advance, not after the date.
- She asked to be told when the overrun crossed 10%, which [[Tessa Varga]] did on 2026-08-20.

## What we owe her
- A revised timeline reflecting the 2026-10-15 date, sent 2026-08-20
- The beta report by 2026-11-30, owned by [[Anouk Ferreira]]
- A budget reconciliation with the report
