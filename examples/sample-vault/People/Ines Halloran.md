# Ines Halloran

App lead. Ines owns the [[Skyline Companion App]]: the web dashboard the Bridge serves, the phone app, the export formats, and the beta program with 10 testers that started 2026-08-20.

## Owns
- Companion app, web and mobile
- The local-first data model: readings stay on the Bridge and the phone, no accounts
- Beta feedback triage with [[Anouk Ferreira]]

## Positions she has taken
- At [[2026-04-23-companion-app-kickoff]] she set the "no accounts, no cloud, no telemetry" rule and it has held.
- In [[2026-05-14-switch-to-lora]] she argued to keep Wi-Fi so the station could talk straight to the app without a Bridge. She lost to the field data and accepted the decision in the same meeting, then rewrote the pairing flow. Two of the three beta bugs were in that flow, which she has pointed out herself.
- Asked in [[2026-08-27-launch-readiness-review]] that the launch not ship with the pairing timeout unfixed, even if it means a smaller first batch. Agreed: it is a launch blocker.

## Current commitments
- Fix the subnet pairing timeout by 2026-09-10
- Ship beta build 3 to all 10 testers by 2026-09-12
- Write the calibration screen help text with Anouk by 2026-09-19
- Pull the first four weeks of orchard readings in November for the frost rule

## Ideas she is carrying
- [[Frost Alert for Orchards]]: she wants this to be the first thing built after launch
- [[Community Data Commons]]: supportive, but she wants the privacy model written down before any code

## Working notes
Ines thinks in flows and tests on real people early; the five user interviews in May came from her, and two of the five asked for a station that works where there is no Wi-Fi before the field test proved the point. She is the person most likely to ask "how would someone explain this to a neighbour?" and the answer usually changes the design. Her line from the radio decision: she would rather lose to data than win on preference.
