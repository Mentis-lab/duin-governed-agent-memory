# Halvard Brenna

Owner of Greyfell Assembly, the small contract manufacturer building the Skyline rev C main boards. Halvard runs a twelve-person shop that does PCB assembly for low-volume hardware startups. Kestrel Labs is one of his smaller customers, which matters when parts are scarce.

## Relationship
- Quoted rev C on 2026-06-18 at [[2026-06-18-greyfell-production-review]]: 60 boards at $41, six-week lead time, delivery 2026-07-30, and a list of DFM issues including a mirrored antenna connector footprint that [[Tessa Varga]] fixed by 2026-06-23.
- Built the pilot run of five boards, delivered 2026-07-22. They work.
- Emailed on 2026-07-28 that the LoRa radio module was on allocation and his distributor could not confirm stock. Delivery of the remaining 55 boards moved to 2026-09-05.
- At [[2026-08-06-supplier-delay-escalation]] the date moved again to 2026-09-19, with the module now costing $12 more per board.

## What he has offered
- To build the 55 boards without the radio module and hand-place modules later if Kestrel finds them from a second source
- A 5% discount on the next batch for the trouble
- Weekly stock updates every Friday, which have arrived on time so far

## What we need from him
- Remaining rev C boards by 2026-09-19, or a firm date by 2026-09-08 if that is going to move
- A quote for a 100-board second batch by 2026-09-10, so the follow-up to [[2026-07-02-beta-batch-size]] can be decided on 2026-09-12

## Working notes
Halvard is straightforward and has never hidden bad news, which is why the studio has stayed with him rather than requoting. But he has now given three dates and hit none of them, and the [[Community Launch]] cannot absorb a fourth slip. Tessa's view is that the risk is the module supply chain, not the shop. Every launch item in [[Skyline v1 Hardware]] runs through his delivery date.
