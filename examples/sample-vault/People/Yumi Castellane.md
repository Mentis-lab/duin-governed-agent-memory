# Yumi Castellane

Coordinator of the Brightwater Makers Collective, a network of three community workshops with about 300 members. Yumi is the beta partner for the [[Community Launch]]: her members will build and run the first 40 Skyline kits, and the collective's rooftop was one of the two May field-test sites.

## Relationship
- First contact 2026-03-20, following an action from [[2026-03-12-q2-planning]].
- Hosted the field test 2026-05-02 to 2026-05-07 on the Brightwater rooftop, and connected the studio with the Hollowmere orchard site.
- Planned the beta program at [[2026-07-16-beta-program-planning]]: 40 kits across the three workshops, an onboarding session, and an autumn workshop series starting the week of 2026-10-05.
- Delivered the participant list and site list on 2026-08-01, on time.
