# Lodestar grant call — 2026-04-02

**Attendees:** [[Tessa Varga]], [[Anouk Ferreira]], [[Corinne Abelard]] (Lodestar Open Hardware Fund)
**Purpose:** agree the terms of the Skyline v1 grant.

## Summary
Lodestar will fund the v1 program with **$88,000 in two tranches**. Tranche 1 ($48,000) is confirmed and pays for the beta batch and the board revisions. Tranche 2 ($40,000) depends on a beta report delivered by **2026-11-30** showing at least 30 stations reporting for four consecutive weeks. The fund requires a recognised open hardware license on the design files, which [[2026-03-19-open-source-license]] already covers.

## Discussion
- Corinne asked how many kits the beta would have. Tessa said 40 to 100, depending on cost; Corinne said 30 reporting stations is the bar, so 40 is enough if they all work.
- Corinne asked what happens if the beta is late. Her answer to her own question: she would rather read an honest late report than a rushed one, but a report after 2026-11-30 needs an extension agreed in advance.
- Anouk described the Brightwater Makers Collective as the beta partner. Corinne liked that the testers are not the studio's friends.
- Budget: Tessa presented a $61,000 v1 plan ($48,000 grant plus $13,000 studio reserve). Corinne asked to be told if the overrun passes 10%.

## Decisions taken here
- Grant accepted on the terms above
- Beta report owner: Anouk
