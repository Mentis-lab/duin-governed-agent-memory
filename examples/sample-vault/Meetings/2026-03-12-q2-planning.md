# Q2 planning — 2026-03-12

**Attendees:** [[Tessa Varga]], [[Rafael Nkemdi]], [[Ines Halloran]], [[Anouk Ferreira]]
**Purpose:** set the plan and the target date for the Skyline v1 public release.

## Summary
The studio agreed to aim for a community launch on **2026-09-24**, with a beta of hardware kits going to an outside group before the public release. Three workstreams were named and given owners: [[Skyline v1 Hardware]] (Tessa), [[Skyline Companion App]] (Ines), and [[Community Launch]] (Anouk). Rafael sits under hardware for firmware. Mechanical design has no owner yet; Tessa will look for a part-time contractor.

## Discussion
- Tessa presented the v0 prototype: sensors on a dev board, Wi-Fi to a laptop, battery life measured in hours. Good enough to prove the sensor chain, nothing else.
- Rafael raised power as the first real problem. Nobody knows whether Wi-Fi can be made to last on a solar cell. He will measure before anyone designs.
- Ines wants the app to work with no account and no server. Agreed in principle, to be confirmed at the app kickoff.
- Anouk raised the license question. Nobody wanted to decide it in this meeting; Tessa will draft options.
- Funding: the Lodestar Open Hardware Fund has invited a proposal. Tessa will take the call.

## Decisions taken here
- Launch target 2026-09-24 (later moved, see [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]])
- Three projects, three owners
