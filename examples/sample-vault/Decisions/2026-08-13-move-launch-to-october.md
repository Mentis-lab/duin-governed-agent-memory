# Decision: move the community launch from 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-15

**Date:** 2026-08-13 · **Decided by:** [[Tessa Varga]], [[Anouk Ferreira]], [[Ines Halloran]], [[Rafael Nkemdi]] · **Status:** accepted

## Context
The launch date of 2026-09-24 was set at [[2026-03-12-q2-planning]]. By August it depended on rev C boards that would not arrive before 2026-09-19 ([[2026-08-06-supplier-delay-escalation]]), an enclosure rev 2 that was already tight for 2026-08-29, and an assembly guide waiting on that enclosure. Two fixed points constrain the choice: [[Yumi Castellane]]'s workshop series starts the week of 2026-10-05 and needs kits by 2026-09-30, and [[Corinne Abelard]]'s beta report is due 2026-11-30 with four weeks of station data, so the beta must start by 2026-10-31.

## Options (drafted by Anouk on 2026-08-12)
1. **2026-10-01.** Two weeks after boards arrive. No margin for a failed water test or a third supplier slip; the public repository would go live before the beta members have built anything.
2. **2026-10-15.** Three weeks of assembly and one week of beta before going public. Kits still reach Yumi on 2026-09-30. The report timeline holds with two weeks of margin.
3. **2026-11-05.** Comfortable for hardware, but the beta would start after the workshop series and the 2026-11-30 report would need an extension.

## Decision
Option 2. Community launch on **2026-10-15**. Kit handover to Brightwater stays 2026-09-30. Public orders open as pre-orders on launch day per [[2026-07-02-beta-batch-size]].

## Rationale
Option 1 trades margin the studio does not have for two weeks nobody asked for. Option 3 puts the grant at risk to protect against a slip that has not happened yet. If Greyfell slips a third time, this decision gets revisited rather than pre-empted.

## Consequences
- Tessa told Corinne on 2026-08-14; Corinne accepted and restated the 2026-10-31 beta-start limit.
- Anouk updated the repository roadmap and the [[Community Launch]] project note.
- A third supplier slip, or a failed enclosure water test on 2026-09-03, reopens this decision. Trigger owner: Tessa.
