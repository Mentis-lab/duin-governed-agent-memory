# Kit Tiers

**Proposed by:** [[Yumi Castellane]] (board-only request) and [[Tessa Varga]] (pricing) · **Status:** proposed, decide before pre-orders open on 2026-10-15

## The idea
Sell Skyline in two tiers instead of one. A **full kit** with every part, and a **board-only** tier with the assembled main board, radio and Bridge for people who will print the enclosure and source the sensors themselves. Brightwater members with printers asked for exactly this at [[2026-07-16-beta-program-planning]].

## Numbers (draft)
| Tier | Contents | BOM | Draft price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full kit | Board, Bridge, sensors, enclosure, mast, solar, cell | $162 | $249 |
| Board only | Board, Bridge, antenna | $71 | $119 |

Margins are thin at 40 to 100 units and only work because the design and documentation are already paid for by the grant. See [[Open Hardware Business Models]] for why kits, not boards, usually carry an open hardware studio.

## Why it matters
- It lowers the price of entry for the people most likely to contribute back: the ones who print and hack.
- The [[2026-06-04-enclosure-redesign]] produced printable files anyway, so the tier costs nothing extra to support in principle.
- It makes the second-batch question in [[2026-07-02-beta-batch-size]] harder: 100 boards could be 60 full kits and 40 board-only, which changes the parts order.

## Risks
- Support load. A board-only builder with a third-party sensor will report bugs the studio cannot reproduce. [[Anouk Ferreira]] wants a "supported configurations" page before this tier exists.
- Two SKUs to document, pack and test with a team of five.

## Open questions
- Does board-only include the Bridge, or is the Bridge a third item?
- Is $119 low enough to matter and high enough to cover a support call?
