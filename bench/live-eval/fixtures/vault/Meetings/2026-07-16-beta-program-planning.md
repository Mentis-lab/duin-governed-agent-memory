# Beta program planning — 2026-07-16

**Attendees:** [[Anouk Ferreira]], [[Ines Halloran]], [[Yumi Castellane]] (Brightwater Makers Collective)
**Purpose:** plan the 40-kit beta that precedes the [[Community Launch]].

## Summary
The beta puts 40 Skyline kits with Brightwater members across the collective's three workshops, with an onboarding session and an autumn workshop series starting the week of 2026-10-05. Yumi needs the kits by **2026-09-30**. She was clear that the workshop dates are published to 300 members and will not move; if kits are late she runs with fewer stations.

## Plan
- 40 kits: 18 at the main workshop, 12 at the second site, 10 at the third, including the Hollowmere orchard members
- Members build their own station from the kit using Anouk's assembly guide, in a supervised session
- Each station reports for at least four weeks; that data is the beta report for [[Corinne Abelard]]
- 10 members get the app beta early, from 2026-08-20, to shake out pairing before the full group

## Discussion
- Yumi asked who supports a member whose station fails. Anouk: the studio, through a repository issue and a monthly call. Yumi wants a named person; Anouk will be that person for the beta.
- Ines walked through the pairing flow. Yumi's reaction was that the Bridge step needs a picture, not a paragraph. Noted for the guide.
- Yumi asked for a cheaper board-only option for members with printers. Captured in [[Kit Tiers]].
- The orchard members asked, through Yumi, for a frost alert. Captured in [[Frost Alert for Orchards]].
- Yumi asked that any community data map be opt-in. Anouk agreed; see [[Community Data Commons]].

## Decisions taken here

- Anouk is the named support contact for the beta
