# Community Launch

**Owner:** [[Anouk Ferreira]] · **Launch date:** 2026-10-15 (moved from 2026-09-24 by [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]]) · **Status:** at risk.

The community launch is the moment Skyline becomes public: the hardware files, firmware and app go up under the licenses chosen in [[2026-03-19-open-source-license]], the assembly and calibration guides go live, and 40 beta kits go to members of the Brightwater Makers Collective through [[Yumi Castellane]]. The beta report is what unlocks tranche 2 of the Lodestar grant ([[2026-04-02-lodestar-grant-call]]), so this project carries the studio's funding as well as its reputation.

## What is blocking it (as of [[2026-08-27-launch-readiness-review]])
1. **Rev C boards.** 55 boards, ETA 2026-09-19 from Greyfell Assembly, after two slips. Without them there are no kits. Owner [[Tessa Varga]], supplier contact [[Halvard Brenna]].
2. **Enclosure rev 2.** Due 2026-09-12, owner [[Jonas Wexley]]. Kits cannot ship in the old housing.
3. **Assembly guide.** 60% written. Anouk needs the final enclosure to photograph the steps. Due 2026-09-26, to Yumi by 2026-09-21.
4. **App beta bugs.** Three found, one fixed. The pairing timeout is a launch blocker. Owner [[Ines Halloran]], due 2026-09-10.
5. **Budget.** The v1 plan is 12% over. The decision on a second batch is due 2026-09-12, see [[2026-07-02-beta-batch-size]].

Yumi needs the kits by 2026-09-30 for the autumn workshop series. Boards on 09-19 plus one week of assembly lands on 09-26. Four days of slack. If Greyfell slips a third time, the launch moves again or the workshops run with fewer stations.

## Done
- License chosen (2026-03-19)
- Beta site list and 40 participants confirmed by Yumi (2026-08-01)
- Public repository structure and contributor guide drafted by Anouk (2026-07-30)
- App beta with 10 testers running (2026-08-20)

## Success looks like
40 stations reporting for four weeks, an honest beta report to [[Corinne Abelard]] by 2026-11-30, and at least five outside contributors on the repository by year end. The beta must start by 2026-10-31 for the report date to hold.
