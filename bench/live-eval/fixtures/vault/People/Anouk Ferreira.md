Community and documentation lead, and owner of the [[Community Launch]]. Anouk writes the assembly, calibration and flashing guides, runs the public repository, and is the studio's contact for [[Yumi Castellane]] at the Brightwater Makers Collective.

## Owns
- The community launch, now dated 2026-10-15
- The assembly guide (60% written at 2026-08-27, to Yumi by 2026-09-21, final by 2026-09-26), the calibration guide and the flashing guide

- The beta report that goes to [[Corinne Abelard]] by 2026-11-30

- Drafted the three launch-date options that went into [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]] and recommended the middle one.
- Proposed [[Community Data Commons]] and wants an opt-in privacy model written before code.
