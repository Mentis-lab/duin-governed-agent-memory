# Week 20 — 2026-05-11 to 2026-05-17

## What happened
- The radio decision is made: [[2026-05-14-switch-to-lora]]. Rafael's range test on 2026-05-13 held 1.2 km at Hollowmere; the Wi-Fi station in the same spot never connected in May. The Bridge is now part of the kit.
- [[Ines Halloran]] argued for keeping Wi-Fi, lost, and had the pairing flow redrawn by Friday. She said in the meeting that she would rather lose to data than win on preference. That is the sentence of the week.

## What I learned
The field test ([[2026-05-09-field-test-debrief]]) cost a week and saved the project. Every assumption that broke was one we could have tested in April on a roof. Test on a roof earlier next time.
