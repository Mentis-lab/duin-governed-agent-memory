# Decision: beta batch of 40 kits, second batch deferred to 2026-09-12

**Date:** 2026-07-02 · **Decided by:** [[Tessa Varga]], [[Anouk Ferreira]], [[Rafael Nkemdi]] · **Status:** accepted · **Follow-up decision due:** 2026-09-12

## Context
The Lodestar grant needs at least 30 stations reporting for four weeks ([[2026-04-02-lodestar-grant-call]]). Greyfell quoted 60 boards at $41 and estimated $37 at 100 ([[2026-06-18-greyfell-production-review]]). Kit BOM after the LoRa change is $162. The question was whether to build 40 kits for the beta or 100 kits so that public orders at launch could ship from stock.

## Options
1. **40 kits.** Covers the beta with 10 spare, costs about $6,500 in parts plus the 60-board run. Public orders at launch would wait for a second batch.
2. **100 kits.** Stock for launch day, lower per-board price, about $16,200 in parts. Commits $9,700 more before the beta has proven the design or tranche 2 is confirmed.
3. **40 now, option on more.** Ask Greyfell to hold pricing for a follow-on order to be decided after the boards arrive.

## Decision
Option 3, recorded as: **build 40 kits now**; decide on a second batch of up to 100 boards by **2026-09-12**, when the rev C boards should be in hand and Halvard's volume quote is due (2026-09-10).

## Rationale
Rev C had not been built yet. Building 100 of an untested revision would have turned a design mistake into a $9,700 mistake. The beta only needs 30 working stations. Tranche 2 ($40,000) is the money that should pay for launch stock, and it is not confirmed until the beta report.

## Consequences
- Public orders at launch on 2026-10-15 will be pre-orders, not shipments. [[Anouk Ferreira]] will say so plainly on the launch page.
- The v1 budget stays inside the $13,000 studio reserve, most of which the overrun has since consumed ([[2026-08-27-launch-readiness-review]]).
- The second-batch decision now lands in the same week as the enclosure deadline and the beta bug fixes. See [[Community Launch]] and [[Kit Tiers]].
