# Decision: community launch on 2026-09-24

**Date:** 2026-03-12 · **Decided by:** [[Tessa Varga]], [[Rafael Nkemdi]], [[Ines Halloran]], [[Anouk Ferreira]] · **Status:** superseded by [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]]

## Context
At [[2026-03-12-q2-planning]] the studio needed a public date to plan backwards from: a beta of hardware kits to an outside group first, then the public release of the design files, firmware and app.

## Decision
Community launch on **2026-09-24**, with the beta kits going to the Brightwater Makers Collective before that date.

## Rationale
A date was useful before anyone could defend it. It set the order of work for [[Skyline v1 Hardware]], [[Skyline Companion App]] and [[Community Launch]].

## Consequences
- Every project note carried 2026-09-24 as the launch date until August.
- The rev C board delay ([[2026-08-06-supplier-delay-escalation]]) made the date impossible; it was replaced by [[2026-08-13-move-launch-to-october]], which moved the launch to 2026-10-15.
