## Pre-registered verdicts

- **The fix worked.** DUIN gained 11 points on the identical protocol; the overall gap
  collapsed −9.0 → −1.0, stable across two runs. Temporal reasoning — one of the two
  abilities bitemporal supersession exists for — flipped from a loss to a consistent win
  (+7.7 mean). The governed-distillation stack is no longer losing to naive RAG overall.
- **T1 FIRES on the primary endpoint.** Multi-session mean delta is −7.4, past the
  pre-registered 5-point line, with the SAME sign and magnitude in both runs (no third run:
  the protocol calls one only on sign disagreement). Per pre-registration this prescribes an
  **architecture review of the multi-session path** — a raw-session/whole-session arm for
  spread-evidence queries (retrieval UNITS of sessions, or wider adaptive breadth) — not
  another incremental retrieval patch. The consolidation-writer question (D2) resolves NO.
- **Regression guard:** run 1 single-session-user dipped 13/14 (−7.1 paired, one question);
  run 2 was clean at 14/14. Mean −3.6 is inside the guard line. No threshold tuning owed.

## Hypothesis-generating (explicitly not pre-registered)

- knowledge-update swung ±6.3 symmetrically — judge/arm variance at n=16, reads as parity.
- The residual deficit is now ENTIRELY multi-session-shaped: questions whose evidence spans
  several sessions still favor handing the reader whole sessions. Adaptive breadth widens to
  whole NOTES; the naive arm retrieves whole SESSIONS as units. The architecture review T1
  prescribes should evaluate session-level retrieval units behind the same governed overlay
  (this is the "raw-session store + governed overlay" branch the 2026-08-21 field-evaluation
  tripwire anticipated — arrived at with data, not vibes).
- Scope caveat unchanged from 08-17: synthetic personas; this measures third-party
  long-conversation QA, not fit to the operator. It can falsify retrieval claims; it cannot
  validate the product thesis by itself.
